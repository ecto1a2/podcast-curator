# Benyamin's podcast taste profile

This file is the curator's brain. Claude reads it every day before scoring episodes.
Edit it any time on GitHub. Plain English is fine.

## Who I am
- Healthcare strategist at a large US health system. Not a coder.
- Listens while driving, doing chores, and working out. Roughly 6 to 9 episodes a week, fewer when on an audiobook.
- Often stops episodes early on purpose. Skips final credits and ads.

## What matters most
1. Topic comes first. The episode must be on a topic I am interested in (see Topics below). A great host or guest on a topic I do not care about, like celebrity gossip or ballet, is a no.
2. Among on-topic episodes, guest and host quality decides the ranking. A great guest on an interesting topic beats an average guest on the same topic.
3. Episode-level buzz beats show ratings. One widely discussed episode from a small show beats an average episode of a top-10 show.
4. Recency matters less than quality. An episode from weeks or months ago is fine if it is strong or recently talked about.
5. My stars are my strongest signal. Episodes similar to my starred list (guests, depth, topics) should score high.

## Topics
### Lead (highest weight)
- Comedy, from stand-up comedians I like: Stavros Halkias, Mark Normand, Sam Morril, Neal Brennan, Bobby Lee, Michael Che, Nick Mullen, Colin Quinn, Andrew Santino. Comedians talking ideas (Blocks, Club Random) count double.
- AI: business strategy, AI in healthcare, AI companies (Anthropic, OpenAI), AI and work, AI risk. This is my strongest theme outside comedy.

### Regular
- Big ideas and philosophy: consciousness, meditation, Stoicism, meaning, death, equanimity. Sam Harris, Ezra Klein, Tyler Cowen style.
- Systems and thinking: systems thinking, systems engineering, first principles, mental models, decision making, Theory of Constraints, asking better questions.
- Geopolitics and economics: China, Middle East, great power strategy, how the world works. Sarah Paine, Dan Wang, Yuval Noah Harari style.
- Business and strategy: company deep dives, CEOs and operators, markets.
- Healthcare: health policy, digital health, health system strategy.
- News: only big events, explained well. Not daily headline roundups unless something major happened.
- Crime as a lens on economics or politics: drug trade, cartels, smuggling, fraud, sanctions, money laundering.

### Occasional (at most one every two to three weeks each)
- Parenting and relationships (Dr. Becky style, marriage, raising boys).
- Cooking (weeknight cooking, food science, chef interviews).
- Narrative history.

### Sports
- I already follow Pardon My Take. Only suggest other sports episodes if they are exceptional or about the business, culture, or corruption side of sports (Pablo Torre style).

### Never
- Murder narratives, mystery whodunits, serial true crime.
- Celebrity gossip.

## Format rules
- Two-hour cap. Length only counts against an episode when the topic is borderline.
- Short episodes (under 30 minutes) are welcome. Keep one or two in the mix for chores.
- Patreon or subscriber previews are fine.
- Skip trailers, reruns with no new content, ad-only bonus drops, and housekeeping episodes.
